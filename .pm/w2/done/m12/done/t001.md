## Objective

Design and implement phase-aware startup waiting.

## Context

Fix the confirmed adapter defect in [original 012](../source-012.md). See
[captured evidence](../../../evidence/012-startup-timeout.md) and [milestone scope](../README.md).
The startup failure is established independently of the external scan attribution.

## Files

- `src/muse-sdk.ts`
- `src/muse-sdk-host.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Replace the blanket 20-second acquisition/setup/submission cutoff with an explicit lifecycle-aware waiting policy. Record cold-start and concurrent-start observations and justify the default, configuration and deadline ownership; merely selecting a larger unexplained constant is insufficient.
- [x] Track initialization, session preparation, submission begun and turn acknowledged separately. An allowed slow initialization or session restore can complete without being killed by the old aggregate budget.
- [x] Caller cancellation and shutdown remain effective throughout startup; deadline expiry prevents late startup continuations from submitting a turn. Cleanup stays bounded and closes owned processes through public SDK lifecycle APIs.

## Out of scope

permission bypass defaults, automatic ambiguous-turn replay, paid providers and publication.
